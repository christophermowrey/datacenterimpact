# UI Review Checklist

Use this as a preference and usability review, not a strict automated gate. Record the date, viewport, reviewer, observation, and decision for any item that needs a change.

## Composition

- Does the map remain visually stable while the results list scrolls?
- Is the relationship between map selection and list selection easy to understand?
- Does the page begin in a neutral state rather than emphasizing one facility?
- Is the map legend useful without duplicating the filter controls?
- Does the expanded inventory feel informative rather than visually crowded?

## Search And Results

- Is Address Impact clearly the primary action?
- After a search, is it clear whether results are distance-sorted, text-filtered, or showing the full inventory?
- Can a user recover comfortably from no results or an approximate location?
- Are preliminary and approximate records understandable without dominating the homepage?
- Do list rows remain scannable with long names, lifecycle labels, and score ranges?

## Map

- Do marker colors and shapes distinguish Built, Under construction, Announced, and the searched address at a glance?
- Does the red Under construction treatment remain distinct from Community Impact score colors?
- Are overlapping or nearby markers still selectable and understandable?
- Does the selected facility card obscure too much useful map area?
- Does the map remain useful at 320px, 375px, tablet, and desktop widths?

## Motion And Accessibility

- Does the rotating headline feel calm rather than distracting?
- Does reduced-motion mode feel intentional?
- Are loading, error, filter, and search changes understandable without relying on color?
- Can keyboard and screen-reader users understand the same selected facility and uncertainty context?
- Does the correction action remain discoverable on mobile?
